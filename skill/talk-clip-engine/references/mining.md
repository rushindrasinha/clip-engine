# Mining the moments

Read `transcript.md` end to end. A short is a standalone moment of 20 to 60 s (max 75) that opens on a hook and lands a payoff.

Good candidates: a story with a turn, a contrarian line, a surprising number, advice a 20 year old would save, a funny exchange, an emotional beat.
Skip: anything that needs the slide behind the speaker to make sense, anything that starts with "so as I was saying", anything that only works with the three minutes before it.

## Content pack (one markdown file per source)
- 5 line summary of the source.
- Ranked candidates. For each: number, in and out time, the verbatim hook line, one line on why a stranger would follow for more, platform fit, flags.
- 10 verbatim pull quotes.
- **Misheard words list**: every proper noun, acronym, number and non-English line Whisper got wrong, with the correct form. The render step must apply every one.
- **Forbidden ranges**: any stretch where the source shows someone else's video, a sponsor card, music-only intro or outro.
- **Flags**: named people, regulators, company or athlete numbers that need clearance, IP or piracy angles, family and medical, anything dated ("now", "this year", old prices). A flag means a human decides. It never means you quietly rewrite.

Overlapping candidates are fine when the angle really differs (one cut opens on the number, one on the conflict). For the top three, render an ALT with a different opening line or title band. Instagram Trial Reels make that A/B test cheap.

Multi-camera or edited sources (conference videos): detect shot changes and track the face per shot, exclude bumpers and lower thirds you do not want, never let a crop land on an empty stage or the audience. Joint talks: mark which speaker each short is, and never put a co-speaker's line under your own name.
