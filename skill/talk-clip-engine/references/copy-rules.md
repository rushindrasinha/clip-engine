# Copy rules

Write like the speaker, not like a marketing team.

- **On-screen title**: 3 to 7 words, the hook. `*highlight*` one word. Quote or compress what is said; do not add claims.
- **YouTube Shorts title**: under 70 characters, curiosity first, no clickbait the clip does not pay off.
- **Instagram caption**: 2 to 3 short lines, plain words, then 3 to 5 hashtags. One soft reason to follow, varied between posts, never the identical line twice.
- **YouTube description**: one sentence on where the clip is from, then `#shorts` and 2 or 3 tags.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Short declarative lines. Concrete nouns.
- Every number and claim traces to the transcript. If the talk is old, say nothing that sounds current ("this year", "now") unless it is still true.
- Credit the source in the video (the corner credit) and in the caption when it is a conference talk.
- Flagged shorts say so in a `flags` field in the review sheet so the approver sees it next to the copy.
